Sculptor & Artist

Sourabh Mazumdar

Born on 11 October in Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh, Sourabh Mazumdar is a sculptor whose artistic journey has been shaped by a sustained engagement with form, material and the expressive possibilities of sculpture.

Mazumdar began his formal education in art at the Government College of Arts & Crafts, Lucknow University, where he studied Fine Arts with a focus on Sculpture and Drawing from 1988 to 1993. He subsequently pursued postgraduate studies in sculpture at the College of Art, University of Delhi, completing his M.F.A. (Post Graduate Diploma) between 1998 and 2000.

An important chapter in his artistic development came in 1997, when he was invited to Switzerland by the Margaret Arp Foundation, with sponsorship from Pro Helvetia, Switzerland, to deepen his understanding of marble sculpture. This experience brought him into direct engagement with the traditions and techniques of working in marble and marked an important international dimension in his sculptural practice.

His work has been exhibited extensively since the late 1980s. Early participation included the Very Special Arts of India in New Delhi (1989), the All India Art Exhibition in Varanasi (1991), and annual exhibitions at the Government College of Arts & Crafts, Lucknow. His work was subsequently presented at institutions and exhibitions in Allahabad, New Delhi, Kolkata and elsewhere, including exhibitions associated with the Rajiv Gandhi Foundation, Lalit Kala Academy, AIFACS, the Allahabad Museum, the Birla Academy of Art & Culture, and the College of Art, New Delhi.

His international experience expanded in the late 1990s. In 1998, he participated in an exhibition of sculpture organised by the Scuola di Scultura di Peccia at Teatro Dimitri in Verscio, Switzerland, connecting his Indian artistic training with the European sculptural environment. His works have subsequently entered collections in Switzerland, France, Japan, Belgium and Australia, as well as in New Delhi, Allahabad, Kolkata and other private collections in India and abroad.

Mazumdar has also presented a number of solo exhibitions, including at the Uttar Pradesh Lalit Kala Academy, Lucknow (1993), Triveni Kala Sangam, New Delhi (1996), the Academy of Fine Arts, Kolkata (1998) and the Birla Academy of Art & Culture, Kolkata (1999). His group exhibitions include presentations at Nirala Art Gallery, Allahabad, Lalit Kala Academy, Ravindra Bhavan, New Delhi, and the Birla Academy of Art & Culture, Kolkata.

Across his career, Sourabh Mazumdar's sculptures have been distinguished by an exploration of physical form and material presence. His artistic practice reflects both the rigorous foundation of Indian academic art education and an openness to international approaches to sculpture. His experience with marble in Switzerland, together with his longstanding engagement with sculpture in India, forms an important part of an artistic journey that has extended across several decades and countries.

Today, his works form part of institutional and private collections in India and abroad, reflecting the international reach of his practice and his continuing contribution to contemporary Indian sculpture.
